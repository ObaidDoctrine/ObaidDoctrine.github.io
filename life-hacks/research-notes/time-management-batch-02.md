# Research notes — Time Management batch 02

## Topic: The Eisenhower Matrix: Urgent vs Important
- Use as a practical prioritisation framework, not a validated psychometric instrument.
- Distinguish urgency (time sensitivity/deadline) from importance (meaningful outcomes/responsibilities).
- Do not claim that the framework is proven to improve outcomes by itself.
- Context source: Aeon et al. (2021), meta-analysis of time-management research, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0245066
- Context source: Patzak et al. (2025), systematic review, https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1623228

## Topic: How to Stop Procrastinating on Important Tasks
- Turner & Hodis (2023), systematic review of 21 academic-procrastination intervention studies; 17 reported significant reductions: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09838-x
- Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2024), meta-analysis across 642 independent tests of implementation intentions: https://doi.org/10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563
- Malouff & Schutte (2019), meta-analysis of RCTs: https://doi.org/10.1002/jcad.12243
- Scope caution: academic procrastination findings should not automatically be generalized to every adult, task, or clinical context. Avoid diagnosing laziness or a mental-health condition.
