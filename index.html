HOW WE APPROACH KNOWLEDGE

Evidence, context and clarity come before certainty.

Psychological ideas are easier to understand when we separate evidence from interpretation and popular claims.

01 • CHECK THE EVIDENCE

We aim to distinguish research findings from interpretations, anecdotes and viral claims.

02 • KEEP THE CONTEXT

Human behaviour rarely has one universal explanation; population, setting and circumstances matter.

03 • KEEP THE BOUNDARY CLEAR

Educational content and self-reflection tools are not diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

04 • UPDATE WHEN NEEDED

Important information can change, so content is reviewed and updated when evidence or context warrants it.
