Better understanding starts with better questions.

Obaid Doctrine is an educational knowledge platform focused on psychology, human behaviour, relationships, family and society.

Our purpose

We translate complex ideas into clear, accessible explanations while trying to preserve the uncertainty and context that real research contains.

Editorial standard

Psychology is a scientific discipline with broad applications. For factual claims, our intended editorial approach is to check reputable research and institutions, distinguish evidence from opinion, and avoid turning a complex finding into a universal rule.

The American Psychological Association describes evidence-based practice as integrating the best available research with clinical expertise and the characteristics, culture and preferences of the person receiving care.

What we do not claim

Educational articles are not a substitute for assessment, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified professional. We do not use generic online content to diagnose individuals.
