MEMBER PRIVACY

Privacy & Data

This page explains how data used by your private member area is handled. It is a member-facing summary; the full site policy contains the broader privacy terms.

Your member data is private. Your profile, saved articles, test history, learning progress and reflection journal entries are intended to provide the member features you request. These records are protected by account-based access controls.

What member data may be stored

Depending on the features you use, your account may contain your email address, profile information, preferred language, test results, saved articles, learning progress, member-content completion records and private reflection journal entries.

How it is used

This information is used to provide account access, save your learning activity, show your own results and saved content, maintain your progress, and provide member features you choose to use. Private journal entries and personal test history are not intended to be published to other members.

Personalized recommendations

Member recommendations use your activity on this platform—such as completed tests, saved articles and learning paths—to suggest educational activities. Recommendations do not infer or assign psychological traits, and they are not psychological assessments.

Advertising

Private member activity is not used for advertising personalization by the member features described here. Google AdSense is not currently enabled on Obaid Doctrine. Any future advertising technologies will be handled according to the site's privacy policy and applicable consent requirements.

Your controls

You can update available profile and language settings from Account Settings. You can also request permanent account deletion through the secure account-deletion option provided in Settings. Deletion is handled through a server-side process rather than by trusting a client-side database operation.
