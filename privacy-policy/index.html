Welcome to Obaid Doctrine.

Obaid Doctrine is an independent educational platform focused on psychology, human behaviour, relationships, family and society. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit or contact our website.

You can browse our website without creating an account. If you contact us, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address and message.

Our website may also receive limited technical information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited and general usage information, depending on the services operating on the website.

2. How We Use Information

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Improve our website and content.
  • Understand general website performance and usage.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security or advertising purposes. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to help us understand website traffic and usage.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may be affected if cookies are disabled.

4. Google Analytics

Obaid Doctrine currently uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand general website traffic, page usage and performance. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from network information, device and browser information, and interactions with the website. We use this information for analytics and website improvement.

We do not use Google Analytics to intentionally collect information that directly identifies you. We do not knowingly send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

You can learn more about Google Analytics privacy practices and available controls from Google's Privacy Policy. Where applicable, you can also use Google's Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

If you create an Obaid Doctrine member account, we may process information needed to provide account and member features, such as your email address, profile information, test results, saved articles, learning progress and reflection-journal entries, depending on the features you use.

Information submitted through member features is used to provide those features and is not sold by Obaid Doctrine.
