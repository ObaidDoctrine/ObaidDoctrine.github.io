Research and content standards / تحقیق اور مواد کے اصول

OBAID DOCTRINE aims to explain psychological concepts in plain English and Urdu, distinguish established evidence from hypotheses and popular claims, cite original or authoritative sources, and avoid overstating what a single study can prove. Any future self-reflection tool should be clearly described as educational unless it is validated and appropriately reviewed for a specific use.
