01COMMON BELIEF

“One behaviour tells you what a person is really like.”

A single behaviour can matter, but it rarely gives enough context to explain a whole person or situation.

02WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUGGESTS

Behaviour needs context before it becomes a conclusion.

Observation, interpretation and conclusion are different steps. Expectations, experience and circumstances can shape how an event is understood.

Explore the evidence →
03A BETTER QUESTION

“What evidence, context or alternative explanation am I missing?”

Separating observation from interpretation can make everyday reasoning about people more careful.
