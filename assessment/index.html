Our assessment standards

Clear labels help visitors understand what each tool can—and cannot—tell them.

Educational MCQs

Check knowledge or practise a skill. Scores describe only the answers in that set and should not be treated as a validated measure of ability.

Self-reflection questionnaires

Help people think about everyday tendencies. They are not clinical instruments, diagnoses or guaranteed measures of a stable trait.

Validated psychological assessments

Use this label only when evidence, appropriate population norms, administration requirements, permissions and relevant professional oversight support the specific intended use.

Official selection information

Country-specific requirements should be linked to official sources and checked for currency. The platform is independent and cannot guarantee recruitment outcomes.

Content policy: original questions and explanations; respect copyright and test security; cite official selection requirements; avoid misleading diagnostic claims; design English and Urdu material for their intended audiences.
