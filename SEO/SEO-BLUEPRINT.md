OBAID DOCTRINE — BILINGUAL SEO BLUEPRINT
Prepared: September 2026

POSITIONING
Obaid Doctrine is an educational knowledge platform about psychology, human behaviour,
relationships, family and society. It should not present itself as a clinical practice unless
appropriate professional credentials and services are actually provided.

SEO PRINCIPLES
1. Build topic depth around a small number of clear subject areas.
2. Write for people first; do not create pages just to target keywords.
3. Verify factual psychological claims against credible research and reputable institutions.
4. Give every article a clear author/byline, publication date and reviewed/updated date where appropriate.
5. Use English and Urdu as separate language URLs and connect equivalent pages with hreflang.
6. Avoid keyword stuffing, copied/rephrased articles and unsupported psychological claims.
7. Use internal links between pillar pages and supporting articles.
8. Use descriptive URLs, unique titles and useful meta descriptions.
9. Submit the XML sitemap in Google Search Console after the final domain is live.
10. Treat SEO as a long-term content system; rankings are not guaranteed.

ON-PAGE TEMPLATE FOR EVERY ARTICLE
- One clear H1 matching the user question/topic.
- 1–2 sentence direct answer near the top.
- Clear H2 sections that cover the search intent completely.
- Evidence section: distinguish established evidence from interpretation.
- Practical section: safe, realistic takeaways.
- “What the research does NOT prove” section when a viral claim is likely.
- Author/byline and publication date.
- References to primary research/reputable institutions where factual claims matter.
- 3–6 relevant internal links.
- 1–3 relevant external authoritative links when useful.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- Unique title tag and meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Article structured data only when the page qualifies for it.

EDITORIAL TRUST RULES
- Never diagnose a reader from a general article.
- Never promise a psychological technique will work for everyone.
- Do not use “science proves” for a single study.
- Separate correlation from causation.
- Mention study limitations when they materially affect the conclusion.
- Avoid sensational mental-health labels.
- For high-stakes mental-health content, use authoritative health guidance and a professional-care disclaimer.

INTERNAL LINKING MODEL
Pillar page → 4–8 supporting articles.
Each supporting article → pillar page + 2–4 related articles.
New article → link to at least 2 older relevant pages.
Update older articles when new evidence changes an important conclusion.

SOURCE PRIORITY
Tier 1: peer-reviewed systematic reviews/meta-analyses, major health/public institutions, original research.
Tier 2: professional associations such as APA.
Tier 3: reputable universities and research centers.
Tier 4: high-quality secondary reporting.
Social posts, blogs and viral graphics should not be treated as evidence without verification.

INITIAL RESEARCH SOURCE BASE
- American Psychological Association (APA): psychology topics and evidence-based practice.
- World Health Organization (WHO): mental health and social connection.
- Primary peer-reviewed research should be added article-by-article.
