## Cannibalization / overlap watchlist
These are **potential overlaps**, not confirmed cannibalization. Confirm using Search Console query-to-page data before changing URLs or combining content:
1. English and Urdu equivalents are intentionally separate language targets; do not merge them. Keep language-specific query phrasing.
2. `/articles/why-couples-misunderstand/` vs any future “why couples keep misunderstanding” article in `SEO/content-plan.json`: the planned idea overlaps an existing live page. Do not publish a second page targeting the same intent without a clearly distinct angle.
3. `/articles/emotional-intelligence/` vs planned “signs of strong emotional intelligence” and “emotional intelligence vs IQ”: these can be a pillar + distinct supporting questions, but each must have a unique query intent.
4. `/articles/cognitive-biases-everyday-decisions/` vs planned “confirmation bias” and “cognitive dissonance”: distinguish the broad cognitive-bias overview from individual concepts.
5. `/articles/what-is-anxiety/` vs proposed anxiety symptom/stress-comparison pages: keep the definition page distinct from comparison or symptom-specific intent.
6. `/articles/how-to-digital-detox/` vs proposed “hard to stop scrolling” and “keep checking phone” pages: separate detox guidance from habit-mechanism questions.
7. `/articles/self-awareness/` and `/mind-tests/self-awareness/`: article targets the meaning/explanation intent; the test page should target a self-awareness assessment/test intent, not duplicate the article title target.
