RESEARCH METHOD

Five questions for a better evidence check.

1. What was the question?

What did the study actually test? Did the headline make a narrower finding sound broader?

2. Who was studied?

Look at the population and sample. A result from one group does not automatically describe everyone.

3. What was the design?

Experiment, survey, observation or another design? The method affects what conclusions are justified.

4. How strong is the result?

Statistical significance alone does not tell the whole story. Consider effect size, uncertainty and alternative explanations.

5. What are the limits?

Every study has boundaries. Context and limitations matter before a finding becomes a broad life rule.
